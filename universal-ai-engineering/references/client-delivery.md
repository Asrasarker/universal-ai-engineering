# Client delivery and handoff

Use this for client engagements, substantial cross-team delivery, or transfers of operating ownership. Reuse the existing statement of work, backlog, decision log, and reporting format when available. Scale the following work and evidence to the engagement.

## Establish the outcome and decision rights

- Define the user or business problem, sponsor, affected users, outcome metric, baseline, target, measurement window and source, and accountable owner. Record how to collect a missing baseline. Identify assumptions, scope and exclusions, deadlines, constraints, and acceptance criteria. Validate assumptions with users, usage evidence, or a pilot where feasible. [Thoughtworks discovery and inception example](https://info.thoughtworks.com/rs/199-QDE-291/images/Preventive%20Approach.pdf)
- For consequential decisions, present viable options with expected value, cost or effort, time, delivery risk, dependencies, and reversibility. Recommend one and explain the trade-off that drives the choice. Label estimates with assumptions and uncertainty. Record the decision-maker, decision deadline, and downstream effects. Surface scope, cost, or timeline changes before making commitments.
- Plan in usable increments with milestones and dependencies. Assign an owner for each external dependency and escalation. In status updates, show the current milestone, what was demonstrated, risks and forecast changes, and the next decision or action. State the impact and due date for each decision. Do not infer acceptance from silence.

## Verify acceptance and adoption

- Agree who accepts each deliverable and how acceptance is demonstrated. Validate the technical criteria through engineering checks and the business criteria through authorized user acceptance testing with representative users or delegates. Capture outcomes, evidence, open items, and actual acceptance decisions. Record a pending approver as pending.
- For changes to how people work, plan pilot or rollout cohorts, communications, training, support channels, and feedback. Measure adoption and the agreed business outcome after release. If the outcome takes time to measure, report it as pending with the review date and owner. [McKinsey on product teams and user adoption](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/tech-and-ai/our-insights/products-and-platforms-is-your-technology-operating-model-ready)

## Transfer ownership

- Identify the receiving owners for code and builds, environments and accounts, support, incident response, data, vendors, and billing as applicable. Document access and transfer requirements without embedding secrets. Confirm the owner can find the architecture and operating instructions, build or release a change in the approved environment, respond to an alert, and locate the recovery procedure. Use a witnessed walkthrough or a rehearsal when practical and record the outcome. [Thoughtworks on transferring ownership and context](https://www.thoughtworks.com/en-au/insights/blog/introduction-agile-knowledge-transfer)
- Deliver a short sponsor summary with the outcome and its evidence, scope delivered, what is pending, residual risks, decisions needed, and next milestone. Link the exact release and acceptance records. Provide the receiving team with the relevant runbooks, support or escalation information, and ownership register. Agree on the stabilization period, support responsibilities, and exit criteria when the engagement calls for them.
